💛 Cute Mode

A single toggle in Settings — from clean to adorable
“Every heart has a job.” Hearts serve as backdrops, markers, separators, and brand stamps — never random decoration. UX first, cute second.

Clean (Cute Mode OFF)

No hearts at all. Standard dots, lines, and circles — a straightforward, minimal UI with zero heart imagery. Clean means clean.

Adorable (Cute Mode ON)

Hearts woven into the UI. Chart dots become heart-shaped markers. Timeline bullets become colored hearts (💙 water, 💛 pee). Widget buttons get heart-shaped backgrounds. Clock tick marks turn into tiny hearts. A gentle watermark heart sits at the center of the clock face. Every heart has a job — framing, marking, branding — never just floating. UX first, cute second.
