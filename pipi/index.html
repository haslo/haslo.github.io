⚡ TL;DR

Pipi is a water + pee tracker for iOS. You log water in, you log pee out, and the app tells you whether you're actually hydrated — not just drinking.

The Hydration Cycle Ring — hero widget
  • Water in, pee out. The only app that tracks both sides of hydration.
  • Hydration Proof Engine. Correlates intake with output to calculate a real hydration score.
  • Widget-first. Interactive home screen widgets — log without opening the app.
  • 100% cute, zero clinical. Emoji urgency levels, optional heart accents (every heart has a job — backdrops, markers, never random decoration), no “voiding” anywhere.
  • No subscription. Core logging free forever. One-time premium for widgets (~$5-8).
  • HealthKit native. Reads and writes water data. Part of the Gwen Widgets ecosystem, sharing HealthWidgetsCore.
