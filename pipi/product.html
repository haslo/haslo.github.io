🎯 Vision & Positioning

Most hydration apps tell you to drink more water. Pipi tells you whether it's actually working. Track your water intake with one tap, log your bathroom visits with a single emoji, and let the Hydration Proof Engine connect the dots. Beautiful interactive widgets mean you rarely need to open the app at all.

Target audience: Health-conscious iPhone users who want effortless hydration tracking without subscription fatigue.

Name rationale: "Pipi" is playful and internationally understood — it works in German, French, Spanish, and English. It's cute, memorable, and sets the tone: this app takes hydration seriously but never takes itself seriously.

⭐ Core Features

💧

Water Tracking

  • Input in dl (metric) or fl oz (imperial), user-configurable
  • Preset quick-log buttons — e.g., 2dl glass, 3dl cup, 5dl bottle — user-configurable sizes and labels
  • Water only — no beverage hydration index, no coffee/tea ratios, no "does soup count?" debates. You log water. Simple.
  • HealthKit read + write (dietaryWater) — full interop with other apps
  • Daily goal user-configurable (default 2.5L)
😊

Pee Tracking (Pipi)

100% cute, zero clinical language. No "voiding", no "urination", no color charts.

😰 Urgent 😊 Normal 😌 Just in Case
  • One-tap logging — timestamp auto-captured, that's it
  • Optional fun "how was it?" quick reaction (never required, keep it light)
  • App-local storage only — HealthKit has no urination category (only bladderIncontinence which is medical/symptom, not what we want)
🔔

Notifications & Reminders

  • Smart hydration reminders that calculate your pace — only nudge when falling behind, stop when on track. After your first week, reminders adapt to your day patterns — no 6am nudge on Sundays just because that's your weekday rhythm.
  • Configurable quiet hours (sleep, meetings)
  • Actionable notifications: tap "Log 2.5dl" directly from the banner (UNNotificationAction + AppIntent)
  • Morning summary: yesterday's hydration score + today's goal
  • Achievement notifications: streak days, personal records
  • Optional pee reminder: "It's been a while — bathroom break?"
⌚

Apple Watch

  • Complication showing daily progress (water intake / goal)
  • Quick-log buttons on watch face
  • Pee logging with urgency emoji selection
  • Haptic reminders synced with iPhone schedule
📱

App (Main Screen) — Always Free

  • Clean, easy-on-the-eyes design with quick-log buttons front and center
  • Logging through the app is always free — fast and frictionless
  • Daily timeline: water drops 💧 and pee emoji interleaved, showing the rhythm of your day
  • Simple charts: daily water intake, pee frequency, hydration score
  • Pattern insights: “You have 2 distinct hydration patterns” — tap to compare your typical Monday vs your typical Saturday
  • Settings: configure presets, daily goal, units (dl/oz), Cute Mode toggle
💛

Cute Mode

Toggle between Clean and Cute in Settings. Clean mode: no hearts at all — straightforward, minimal UI. Cute mode: heart-shaped backdrops behind icons, heart markers on chart lines and timelines, heart tick marks on clocks — 💛 yellow for pee, 💙 blue for water. Every heart has a job; none just float. UX first, cute second.

🚀 USPs — What Makes Pipi Different

1

Hydration Proof Engine

Correlates water intake with pee frequency to show whether you're actually hydrated, not just drinking. "You drank 2.5L but only went twice today — drink more!" No competitor combines both data streams intelligently. This is the app's defining feature. Over time, the engine learns your personal hydration patterns — it knows your body processes water differently on active days vs rest days.

2

Smart Reminders That Stop

Reminders pause automatically when you're on track for your daily goal. The #1 complaint about Plant Nanny, WaterMinder, and every other water app: they keep nagging after you've hit your target. Pipi calculates your pace and only nudges when you're falling behind. Better yet, after a week it learns your day types — the Thursday-at-the-office reminder schedule adapts separately from Sunday-on-the-couch.

3

Zero Paywall on Logging

All tracking features free forever. Open the app, tap, done. Interactive widgets are premium (convenience upgrade, not feature gate). The #1 App Store complaint across ALL water apps is subscription paywalls on basic logging. Pipi has no subscription at all.

4

One-Tap Home Screen Logging

Interactive widgets, Control Center buttons, and Lock Screen actions mean you can log water or pee without ever opening the app. The widget IS the app for daily use.

5

Water AND Pee in One App

Nobody does both. Water apps ignore output. Pipi tracks the full cycle — because hydration isn't just about drinking, it's about what your body does with it. Heart accents (💛💙) woven into the UI — as icon backdrops, data markers, clock tick marks — make pee tracking feel cute and approachable instead of clinical.

6

User-Configurable Presets

Your glass, your bottle, your mug — configured once, always one tap away. No scrolling through preset lists that don't match your life.
