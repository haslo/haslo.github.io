🔄 The Full Circle

Water goes in. Pee comes out. That's the cycle — and that's the design. Every core visualization is a circle, because hydration is a circle.

Hydration Cycle Ring

The hero widget · App icon candidate

Dual-arc circle. Blue fills clockwise for water intake (% of daily goal). Yellow fills counter-clockwise for pee frequency (vs your own 7-day rolling average — no medical norms, your pattern is your baseline). Both complete → ring turns green.

Small Medium Watch

The Pipi Clock

24-hour circular timeline

Clock-face layout showing your hydration day as a circle. Blue water drops at logged times around the outside, pee emoji at logged times around the inside. See your daily rhythm at a glance.

Medium Large

Activity Rings

Apple Watch style · Familiar UX

Three concentric rings: Water (outer, blue), Pipi frequency (middle, yellow), Hydration Score (inner, green). Close all three to complete your hydration day. Watch complication uses this same visual.

Small Medium Watch

Streak Circle

30-day motivation ring

30 segments, one per day. Green = hit hydration goal, empty = upcoming, red = missed. Complete the full circle for a 30-day streak celebration. Today's segment pulses blue while in progress.
