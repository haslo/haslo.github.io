⚔ Combat — Actions & Economy
Your Turn: Move + Action + Bonus Action (BA) + Free
Attack Melee or ranged. d20 + Prof + STR/DEX mod ≥ AC → hit. Nat 20 = crit. Nat 1 = auto-miss.
Magic Cast spell, use magic item/feature. Only one spell slot/turn (cantrips free).
Dash Gain extra movement equal to your Speed this turn.
Disengage Your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks this turn.
Dodge Attacks against you have Disadv. Adv on DEX saves. Ends if Speed = 0.
Help Ally within 5 ft gains Adv on next check (you must have Prof in that skill) or attack roll.
Hide DC 15 DEX (Stealth). ¾/total cover or heavy obscurement → Invisible.
Ready Set trigger + response (action/move). Uses Reaction. Spells need Conc.
Search WIS (Perception) or INT (Investigation). Find hidden creatures, objects, clues.
Study INT (Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion). Recall lore, examine creature/object.
Influence CHA (Persuasion/Deception/Intimidation), WIS (Animal Handling). DC 10 favor · DC 20 risk/sacrifice.
Utilize Use a nonmagical object, activate an item, or interact with environment.
Turn Flow
Heroic Inspiration — DM rewards compelling roleplay, adhering to character, creative/heroic actions — player spends to reroll any die immediately after rolling. Nat 1 grants one. Max 1 (or pass on).
Opportunity Attack (OA) enemy leaves your reach → one melee attack as a Reaction  ·  Readied Action your set trigger fires, uses your Reaction  ·  Reaction Spells Shield, Counterspell, etc.  ·  Free Interaction draw or stow a weapon per attack · open a door · pick up an item · hand something to an ally
Combat Extras
  • Critical Hit — Nat 20: double all damage dice. Always hits.
  • Unarmed Strike — 1 + STR bludg. Or Grapple/Shove: target STR/DEX save vs 8+STR+Prof.
  • Two-Weapon Fighting — both Light. BA for 2nd attack (no mod to dmg unless Nick).
  • Potion — drink or administer: Bonus Action.
Surprise & Initiative
  • Initiative — d20 + DEX mod. Ties: DM decides monsters, players decide PCs.
  • Surprise — Disadv on Initiative. Still act normally on their turn.
  • Hidden at combat start → Adv on Initiative (via Invisible).
Weapon Masteries
Cleave — hit → free attack on adj. creature (same mod, no extra dmg dice)
Graze — miss → deal ability mod as dmg anyway
Nick — extra attack as part of Attack action (no BA needed)
Push — hit → push target 10 ft straight away
Sap — hit → target Disadv on next attack before your next turn
Slow — hit → target speed −10 ft, no Reactions until its next turn
Topple — hit → target CON save (8+mod+Prof) or Prone
Vex — hit → Adv on your next attack against same target
🏃 Movement & Terrain
Movement Basics
  • Speed = feet you can move on your turn. Subtract when switching speed types.
  • Difficult Terrain: each foot costs 1 extra foot.
  • Crawling: each foot costs 1 extra foot (2 extra in difficult terrain).
  • Prone: standing up costs half your Speed. Dropping prone is free.
  • Through creatures: allies freely. Hostile = difficult terrain. Can't end in their space.
  • Squeezing: 1 size smaller → difficult terrain + Disadv on attacks + Adv to hit you.
Climbing & Swimming
  • Each foot costs 1 extra foot (2 extra in difficult terrain) unless you have a Climb/Swim speed.
  • Slippery/rough: DM may require STR (Athletics). Rough water: DC 15.

Carrying & Mounted
Carry: STR × 15 lbs. Push/Drag/Lift: STR × 30 lbs (Speed 5 ft). Size mod: ×2 per size above Med, ×½ Small, ×¼ Tiny.
  • Mounting costs half Speed. Mount ≥ 1 size larger, willing.
  • Controlled: your Init, can only Dash/Disengage/Dodge.
  • Forced dismount: DC 10 DEX save or Prone within 5 ft.
🎭 Conditions
Blinded — Can't see. Auto-fail sight checks. Atks Disadv. Atks against Adv.
Charmed — Can't attack/harm charmer. Charmer Adv on social checks.
Deafened — Can't hear. Auto-fail hearing checks.
Frightened — Disadv atks & checks while source visible. Can't approach.
Grappled — Speed 0. Ends if grappler Incap. or forced apart, or target succeeds escape action.
Incapacitated — No actions, bonus actions, or reactions. Concentration broken.
Invisible — Can't be seen. Atks Adv, atks against Disadv — only if attacker can't see you (Truesight/See Invis. negate).
Paralyzed — Incap. Can't move/speak. Auto-fail STR/DEX. 5 ft hit = Crit.
Petrified — Incap. Auto-fail STR/DEX. Resist all dmg. Immune poison/disease.
Poisoned — Disadv on atks & ability checks.
Prone — Crawl only. Disadv atks. Melee vs Adv, ranged vs Disadv. Stand = ½ Speed.
Restrained — Speed 0. Atks Disadv. Atks against Adv. Disadv DEX saves.
Stunned — Incap. Can't move. Auto-fail STR/DEX. Atks against Adv.
Unconscious — Incap. Drop items, Prone. Auto-fail STR/DEX. 5 ft = Crit.

📋 Saves, Spells & Damage
Death Saving Throws
  • At 0 HP → Unconscious. Roll d20 start of each turn.
  • 10+ = success. 9− = failure. 3 successes → Stable. 3 failures → Death.
  • Nat 20 → regain 1 HP, wake up. Nat 1 → 2 failures.
  • Damage at 0 HP → 1 auto failure. Crit = 2 failures. Any healing → reset saves, wake up.
Concentration
  • Only one Concentration spell at a time.
  • On damage: CON save DC = 10 or half damage, whichever higher (max DC 30).
  • Broken by: failed save, Incapacitated, death, casting another Conc. spell.
  • Readied spells always require Concentration to hold — even non-Conc. spells and cantrips. Breaks existing Conc.

Light & Vision
  • Bright Light — normal vision.
  • Dim Light → Lightly Obscured — Disadv Perception (sight).

Advantage (Adv) & Disadvantage (Disadv)
  • Advantage: roll 2d20, take higher. Disadvantage: take lower.
  • If you have both, they cancel (1d20) regardless of how many sources.
