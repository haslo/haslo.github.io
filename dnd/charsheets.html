Appearance & Quirks

Exceptionally large pointed ears. Oversized luminous green eyes — larger than an elf's for her size, somewhere between a bush baby and a luna moth. Wild black hair. Fair skin.

Iridescent dragonfly wings that shimmer with rainbow refractions. Surrounded by a glittering multicolored mist that emanates from her skin, with tiny sparkling motes of light slowly drifting down. The mist makes a faint sparkly chiming sound — defaults to on when she wakes up, toggled at will. The magic reasserts itself naturally; she has to consciously suppress it.

Loves pink — her makeup and clothing make that obvious, but she's a rainbow of colors overall. Dark pink leggings under a shimmering leaf-and-vine upper body armor. A skirt that falls to the knees but is still practical in a fight. Slightly pointed shoes that are absolutely not made for walking, because why would she walk? Carries a wand with a star at the tip.

Backstory

Twinkle is a fairy from the Feywild. She lived in a secluded cave-grotto deep in an enchanted forest, studying fey magic formally — poring over the Weave's structure, cataloguing how it bends differently in fey spaces, approaching it all with a wizard's rigour. She could already do small fey magic (Druidcraft is innate to all fairies — minor nature cantrips, part of what they are), and she was diligent, curious, analytical. Back then her mist shimmered silver, and she dressed formally — proper fey scholar attire, muted tones, everything in its place.

She was particularly drawn to studying the domain of Hyrsam, the Prince of Fools — archfey of revelry, mischief, and chaos. Not as a worshipper, but as a researcher. Chaos magic is the hardest kind to codify, and she found that interesting. She wanted to understand how Hyrsam's influence shapes the Feywild, how chaos and order coexist in the Weave. She even picked up a flute along the way — you can't properly study the Prince of Fools without understanding his music.

One day the magic she was poking at poked back. Not a curse, not a tragedy — more like tuning a harp and accidentally opening a door. Her silver mist turned rainbow. Her spells started doing unexpected things. Wild Magic found her, or she found it, or Hyrsam noticed her and thought it would be funny. She woke up sparkling in colors she'd never made before, and she thought it was fantastic. The formal scholar attire didn't last long after that — within days she was wearing pink, then rainbow, then whatever made her sparkle hardest.

The structured wizard path suddenly felt like reading about swimming instead of jumping in. So she left the cave.

She has the soul of a sorcerer but the mind of a wizard — still curious, still analytical, still fascinated by how magic works. She just channels it through chaos now instead of theory. The Acolyte background reflects her years of devoted study; the Wild Magic reflects what happened when the subject of her research decided to become part of her.
