Using Legacy Content with 2024 Rules

Species (MotM): Ignore printed ASIs — ability score bonuses now come from your Background (PHB 2024, p. 36). All other traits (resistances, darkvision, innate spellcasting) work as written. MotM already uses flexible ASI (+2/+1 any), so the change is minimal.

Subclasses (TCoE / XGtE): All subclasses now start at level 3. TCoE and XGtE Cleric, Sorcerer, and Warlock subclasses originally started at level 1 — shift those features to level 3. Extra Attack from legacy subclasses doesn't stack with other sources (2024 PHB rule: you never gain Extra Attack more than once from any combination of class features).

Renamed Spells (2014 → 2024): Feeblemind → Befuddlement · Branding Smite → Shining Smite · Summon Draconic Spirit → Summon Dragon. Many other spells were redesigned but kept their names; use the PHB 2024 versions.

Eberron Books Compatibility

E:FotA and FoE: Quickstone use 2024 rules natively — no conversion needed. Exploring Eberron and Chronicles of Eberron (Keith Baker, DMs Guild) were written for 2014 rules — apply the same level-shift and ASI rules as TCoE/XGtE above. Cleric and Druid subclasses from these books start at 1st/2nd level; shift to 3rd.

Specific Subclass Notes

Forge Adept (ExE Artificer): Extra Attack at 5th doesn't stack (2024 rule). Ghaal'shaarat references E:RftLW infusion economy — use E:FotA's instead.

Maverick (ExE Artificer): Arcane Breakthroughs reference "the artificer spell list" — use the E:FotA list. Rest is self-contained.

Hexblade (XGtE Warlock): Hex Warrior (CHA for weapons, medium armor, shields) is now built into 2024 Pact of the Blade — the subclass's signature feature is redundant. Pick a 2024 subclass instead.

Twilight, Peace (TCoE Cleric): Very strong — Twilight Sanctuary and Emboldening Bond are among the most powerful features in the game. Expect GM scrutiny.

Order (TCoE Cleric): No heavy armor at levels 1–2 due to the level shift. Plan early AC accordingly.

Storm Sorcery (XGtE Sorcerer): On the weaker side — pick it for flavour, not optimisation.
