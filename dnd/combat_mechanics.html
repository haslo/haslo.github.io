Spell Attacks & Saves — Two Different Paths
Path A — Spell Attack Roll Caster Rolls
Used by: Fire Bolt, Guiding Bolt, Scorching Ray, Eldritch Blast, Inflict Wounds…
Key difference from weapons: Spell damage is literally what the spell description tells you to roll — nothing more. Fire Bolt says 1d10 fire, you roll 1d10. Your INT +4 does not get added. The spell text is the complete recipe. Rare exceptions exist (e.g. Warlock's Agonizing Blast adds CHA to damage).
A monster casts Fireball — who rolls? The players roll their DEX saves. The monster just has a DC.
Path B — Saving Throw Spell Targets Roll
Used by: Fireball, Hold Person, Thunderwave, Web, Fear, Banishment…
Nat 20 on a saving throw? In 2024 rules, nat 20 = auto-success and nat 1 = auto-fail on all d20 tests. But only attack rolls get critical hit bonus damage — saves just succeed or fail.
