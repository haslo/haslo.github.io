Spark
White Dragonborn Paladin · Level 2 · Spell DC 13 · Spell Attack +5
PHB 255
Command
Level 1 Enchantment · Spell
Action · 60 ft · V · Instantaneous

Speak a one-word command to a creature you can see. Target makes a WIS save or follows the command on its next turn.

Approach. Move toward you by the shortest route.
Drop. Drop whatever it's holding, turn ends.
Flee. Spend its turn moving away from you.
Grovel. Fall Prone, turn ends.
Halt. Doesn't move, takes no actions.

Higher slot: +1 target per level above 1.

PHB 247
Bless Conc
Level 1 Enchantment · Spell
Action · 30 ft · V, S, M (holy symbol 5+ gp) · Up to 1 min

Bless up to 3 creatures within range. Whenever a target makes an attack roll or a saving throw before the spell ends, the target adds 1d4 to the roll.

Higher slot: +1 creature per level above 1.

PHB 308
Purify Food and Drink
Level 1 Transmutation · Ritual
Action · 10 ft · V, S · Instantaneous

All nonmagical food and drink within a 5-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within range is purified and rendered free of poison and disease.

Ritual: can cast without spell slot (10 min casting time).

PHB 264
Divine Smite
Level 1 Evocation · Always Prepared
Bonus Action · Self · V · Instantaneous
Immediately after hitting with a Melee weapon or Unarmed Strike

The target takes an extra 2d8 Radiant damage from the attack. Damage increases by 1d8 if the target is a Fiend or Undead.

Free cast: 1/long rest without expending a spell slot.

Higher slot: +1d8 per level above 1.

PHB 108
Lay On Hands
Class Feature · Paladin
Bonus Action · Touch · Pool: 10 HP (5 × Paladin level)

Touch a creature (including yourself) and draw from your pool to restore any number of HP up to the amount remaining in the pool.

Alternatively, expend 5 HP from the pool to remove the Poisoned condition (doesn't also restore HP).

Pool replenishes when you finish a Long Rest.

PHB 209
Dueling
Fighting Style Feat · Level 2
Passive · Prereq: Fighting Style class feature

When you're holding a Melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.

Longsword (1h): 1d8 + 2 (STR) + 2 (Dueling) = 1d8+4 slashing.

PHB 215
Sap
Weapon Mastery · Longsword
On hit · Longsword: 1d8+4 slashing, versatile (1d10+4)

If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.

Automatic on hit — no save, no resource cost. Great for protecting the party.

PHB 215
Slow
Weapon Mastery · Javelin
On hit · Javelin: 1d6+2 piercing, thrown 30/120

If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once with this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.

Good for keeping enemies from closing distance or escaping.

PHB
Breath Weapon
Species Trait · White Dragonborn
Action · Uses: 2/long rest (Prof Bonus)
Choose: 15 ft Cone or 30 ft Line × 5 ft

Each creature in the area makes a DEX save (DC 12). On a failed save, 1d10 Cold damage. Half on a success.

Choose cone or line each time you use it. Damage scales: 2d10 at L5, 3d10 at L11, 4d10 at L17.

Sirius
Rock Gnome Rogue · Level 2 · Spell DC 12 (Rock Gnome cantrips only)
PHB 127
Sneak Attack
Class Feature · Rogue
1/turn · Extra 1d6 damage · Finesse or Ranged weapon

Once per turn, deal an extra 1d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack roll if you're using a weapon with the Finesse or Ranged property and one of these is true:

Advantage. You have Advantage on the attack roll.
Ally adjacent. An ally is within 5 ft of the target and you don't have Disadvantage.

Scales: 2d6 at L3, 3d6 at L5, etc.

PHB 127
Cunning Action
Class Feature · Rogue Level 2
Bonus Action · Every turn · No resource cost

On each of your turns, you can take one of the following actions as a Bonus Action:

Dash. Double your movement this turn.
Disengage. Your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks this turn.
Hide. Make a Stealth check to become Hidden.

Attack + Hide = Sneak Attack next turn via Advantage.

PHB 127
Expertise
Class Feature · Rogue
Passive · Double proficiency bonus on chosen skills

Your proficiency bonus is doubled for ability checks with two skills of your choice that you're proficient in.

Sirius's picks:
Sleight of Hand: +3 (DEX) +4 (2×Prof) = +7
Stealth: +3 (DEX) +4 (2×Prof) = +7

PHB 215
Nick
Weapon Mastery · Dagger
On attack · Dagger: 1d4+3 piercing, finesse/light/thrown 20/60

When you make the extra attack of the Light property, you can make it as part of the Attack action instead of as a Bonus Action. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.

Frees up your Bonus Action for Cunning Action while still getting two dagger attacks.

PHB 215
Vex
Weapon Mastery · Shortbow
On hit · Shortbow: 1d6+3 piercing, range 80/320, two-handed

If you hit a creature with this weapon, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.

Hit once → Advantage on second shot → easy Sneak Attack trigger.

PHB 203
Lucky
Origin Feat
Luck Points: 3/long rest

Luck Points. You have 3 Luck Points. When you make a d20 Test, you can spend 1 Luck Point to give yourself Advantage on the roll.

Against you. When a creature makes an attack roll against you, you can spend 1 Luck Point to impose Disadvantage on that roll.

Points replenish on a Long Rest.

PHB
Gnomish Cunning
Species Trait · Gnome
Passive · Always active

You have Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.

Protects against most mind-affecting magic: charm, fear, hold person, command, etc. Extremely powerful passive.

PHB 299
Mending
Transmutation Cantrip · Rock Gnome
1 minute · Touch · V, S, M (2 lodestones) · Instantaneous

This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn cloak, or a leaking wineskin. As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage.

Can mend magic items but doesn't restore their magic.

PHB 307
Prestidigitation
Transmutation Cantrip · Rock Gnome
Action · 10 ft · V, S · Up to 1 hour

A minor magical trick. Choose one effect:

• Sensory effect (spark, puff of wind, faint sound, odd odor)
• Light or snuff a candle, torch, or small campfire
• Clean or soil an object no larger than 1 cubic foot
• Chill, warm, or flavor up to 1 cu ft of nonliving material for 1 hour
• Color, mark, or symbol on an object/surface for 1 hour
• Trinket or illusory image that fits in your hand, lasts until end of next turn
