PHB 255
Command
Level 1 Enchantment · Spell
Action · 60 ft · V · Instantaneous

Speak a one-word command to a creature you can see. Target makes a WIS save or follows the command on its next turn.

Approach. Move toward you by the shortest route.
Drop. Drop whatever it's holding, turn ends.
Flee. Spend its turn moving away from you.
Grovel. Fall Prone, turn ends.
Halt. Doesn't move, takes no actions.

Higher slot: +1 target per level above 1.

Higher slot: +1 creature per level above 1.

PHB 264

Bonus Action · Self · V · Instantaneous

Free cast: 1/long rest without expending a spell slot.

Higher slot: +1d8 per level above 1.

PHB 108
Lay On Hands
Class Feature · Paladin
Bonus Action · Touch · Pool: 10 HP (5 × Paladin level)

Touch a creature (including yourself) and draw from your pool to restore any number of HP up to the amount remaining in the pool.

Alternatively, expend 5 HP from the pool to remove the Poisoned condition (doesn't also restore HP).

Pool replenishes when you finish a Long Rest.

Passive · Prereq: Fighting Style class feature

When you're holding a Melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.

Longsword (1h): 1d8 + 2 (STR) + 2 (Dueling) = 1d8+4 slashing.

PHB 215

On hit · Javelin: 1d6+2 piercing, thrown 30/120

PHB 127
Sneak Attack
Class Feature · Rogue
1/turn · Extra 1d6 damage · Finesse or Ranged weapon

Once per turn, deal an extra 1d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack roll if you're using a weapon with the Finesse or Ranged property and one of these is true:

Advantage. You have Advantage on the attack roll.
Ally adjacent. An ally is within 5 ft of the target and you don't have Disadvantage.

Scales: 2d6 at L3, 3d6 at L5, etc.

PHB 127
Cunning Action
Class Feature · Rogue Level 2
Bonus Action · Every turn · No resource cost

On each of your turns, you can take one of the following actions as a Bonus Action:

Dash. Double your movement this turn.
Disengage. Your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks this turn.
Hide. Make a Stealth check to become Hidden.

Attack + Hide = Sneak Attack next turn via Advantage.

PHB 127

Sirius's picks:
Sleight of Hand: +3 (DEX) +4 (2×Prof) = +7
Stealth: +3 (DEX) +4 (2×Prof) = +7

PHB 203
Lucky
Origin Feat
Luck Points: 3/long rest

Against you. When a creature makes an attack roll against you, you can spend 1 Luck Point to impose Disadvantage on that roll.

Points replenish on a Long Rest.

PHB 299
Mending
Transmutation Cantrip · Rock Gnome
1 minute · Touch · V, S, M (2 lodestones) · Instantaneous

This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn cloak, or a leaking wineskin. As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage.

Can mend magic items but doesn't restore their magic.

PHB 318
Sorcerous Burst
Evocation Cantrip · Sorcerer
Action · 120 ft · V, S · Instantaneous

Make a ranged spell attack (+5). On a hit, target takes 1d8 damage of a type you choose: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, Psychic, or Thunder.

Exploding dice: If you roll an 8 on any d8, roll another d8 and add it. Max extra d8s = 3 (CHA mod).

Cantrip Upgrade: 2d8 at L5, 3d8 at L11, 4d8 at L17.

Your go-to ranged attack. Choose damage type to exploit vulnerabilities. Innate Sorcery gives Advantage.

PHB 334
Toll the Dead
Necromancy Cantrip · MI: Cleric
Action · 60 ft · V, S · Instantaneous

You point at one creature you can see. The target hears a dolorous bell audible within 10 ft and must succeed on a WIS save or take 1d8 Necrotic damage.

If the target is missing any HP, it takes 1d12 instead.

Cantrip Upgrade: damage increases by one die at L5, L11, L17.

Save-based (no attack roll) — pairs with Mind Sliver's −1d4 to saves. 1d12 on wounded targets is brutal.

PHB 277
Friends Conc
Enchantment Cantrip · Sorcerer
Action · 10 ft · S, M (some makeup) · Up to 1 min

One creature you can see makes a WIS save or has the Charmed condition for the duration. Auto-succeeds if it isn't a Humanoid, if you're fighting it, or if you've cast this on it in the past 24 hours.

Ends if the target takes damage or you attack/force a save. When it ends, the target knows it was Charmed by you.

PHB 298

PHB 317
Sleep Conc
Level 1 Enchantment · Sorcerer
Action · 60 ft · V, S, M (sand / rose petals) · Up to 1 min

Each creature in a 5-ft-radius Sphere centered on a point within range makes a WIS save.

Fail: Incapacitated until spell ends. At end of each of its turns, repeat save — on a second failure, it falls Unconscious until spell ends or it takes damage or someone shakes it awake.

Creatures immune to sleep or Exhaustion auto-succeed. Mind Sliver → Sleep = chef's kiss.

PHB 334
Thunderwave
Level 1 Evocation · Sorcerer
Action · Self · V, S · Instantaneous

Each creature in a 15-ft Cube originating from you makes a CON save. On a fail, 2d8 Thunder damage and pushed 10 feet away. Half damage on success, no push.

Unsecured objects entirely in the Cube are also pushed 10 ft. Thunderous boom audible within 300 feet.

Higher slot: +1d8 per level above 1.

PHB 140
Innate Sorcery
Class Feature · Sorcerer Level 1
Bonus Action · Self · Uses: 2/long rest
Duration: 1 minute

Unleash the simmering magic within. For 1 minute:

• Spell save DC of your Sorcerer spells increases by 1 (DC 13 → 14)
• You have Advantage on attack rolls of Sorcerer spells you cast

Pop this before your biggest spell turn. Stacks with Metamagic.

PHB 140
Font of Magic
Class Feature · Sorcerer Level 2
Sorcery Points: 2 · Regain all on Long Rest

Fuel Metamagic options and create spell slots.

Convert Slot → SP: Expend a spell slot to gain SP equal to the slot's level (no action).
Create Slot: Bonus Action, spend 2 SP to create one 1st-level spell slot. Slot vanishes on Long Rest.

2 SP = 1 Empowered + 1 Twinned, or 1 emergency spell slot.

PHB 142
Empowered Spell
Metamagic Option · Sorcerer Level 2
Cost: 1 Sorcery Point · When you roll damage

When you roll damage for a spell, spend 1 SP to reroll up to 3 damage dice (CHA modifier, minimum 1). You must use the new rolls.

Stackable: You can use Empowered Spell even if you've already used a different Metamagic option on the same spell.

Thunderwave rolled low? Reroll up to 3 of those d8s.

PHB 142
Twinned Spell
Metamagic Option · Sorcerer Level 2
Cost: 1 Sorcery Point · When you cast a spell

When you cast a spell (such as Charm Person) that can be cast with a higher-level spell slot to target an additional creature, spend 1 SP to increase the spell's effective level by 1.

Sleep at effective level 2 (bigger radius?) or Healing Word on two allies with one slot — big value for 1 SP.

MotM
Flight
Species Trait · Fairy
Passive · Fly Speed 30 ft · Always active

You have a flying speed equal to your walking speed (30 ft). You can hover.

If you are flying at the end of your turn and nothing is holding you aloft, you fall.

Twinkle never walks. Use flight to stay out of melee, position for Thunderwave without hitting allies, and deliver Healing Word from safety.
