青年 Seinenoriginally adult-male-targeted Japanese manga. Mature themes — violence, sex, psychological depth, complex stories.
少年 Shōnenoriginally teen-boy-targeted Japanese manga. Action, adventure, friendship-effort-victory tropes. (Naruto, One Piece, DBZ, MHA.)
少女 Shōjooriginally teen-girl-targeted Japanese manga. Romance, emotional drama, often slice-of-life. (Sailor Moon, Fruits Basket, Ouran HSHC.)
女性 Joseioriginally adult-woman-targeted Japanese manga. Realistic romance, workplace drama, slice-of-life with mature register. (Nodame Cantabile, Princess Jellyfish.)
子供 Kodomochildren's Japanese manga. (Doraemon, etc.)
BDBande dessinée — French/Belgian large-format graphic-album tradition. Album-format, painted colour, ~50pp per volume.
Indie GNWestern indie graphic novel. Small-press / creator-owned / Kickstarter tradition.
USAmerican mainstream comics tradition. Marvel, DC, big-Image. Floppy-issue origin, collected into trades / HCs / omnibuses.
The "originally targeted" framing is historical (1960s–70s magazine marketing). Modern manga readership is broadly mixed-gender across all categories — Japan trends ~60% female overall, Western markets even more female-skewing. Treat the labels as publishing-venue taxonomy, not reader-identity prescription.

Series notes
  • What & why: Enki Bilal's foundational FR cyberpunk-dystopia BD — 3 albums 1980 / 1986 / 1992. Alcide Nikopol returns from 30 years of orbital cryo to a fascist alternate-Paris 2023; a hovering Egyptian pyramid hosts the returned pantheon.   • Reading register: FR-original gets read in FR. Bilal's writing is denser-than-conversational, with sustained vocabulary growth on the political-philosophical-SF axis as a side benefit.

Box contents & series notes
  • Premise: 1000 years after the "Seven Days of Fire" industrial-civilization collapse; a toxic fungal forest (Sea of Corruption) slowly consumes the remnant kingdoms. Nausicaä, princess of the small Valley of the Wind, becomes entangled in the genocidal war between the Torumekian and Dorok empires while gradually discovering the Sea of Corruption's engineered purpose and her people's place in the bio-designed post-apocalyptic ecology.
  • Register: grave, ecological, political, post-humanist. Dense specific worldbuilding. Adult-thematic despite young protagonist. Manga is much darker than the 1984 film — diverges severely after the film's ending point and reaches a morally ambiguous post-humanist conclusion the film actively misrepresents. The film covers ~the first quarter of the manga; the back three quarters are the canonical work Miyazaki kept writing for ten more years after the film.
  • Shelf placement: the other foundational 1982 adult-SF magnum opus alongside Akira. Direct ancestor of the cyberpunk-dystopia lineage on the shelf (Akira / Eden / BLAME! / Battle Angel / Tokyo Ghost all post-date or descend from one or both).
  • Edition format: 7 original vols → 4 Doppelband HCs (Doppelband = 2 originals per book; D4 carries the final original vol + epilogue material). Large hardcover Carlsen format, color pages preserved. Slipcase + poster + art print extra. Set ISBN 9783551805102; individual ISBNs 9783551805058 / 9783551805065 / 9783551805072 / 9783551805089.
  • Translation: Jürgen Seebeck (continuous DE Miyazaki/Ghibli translator since the 90s); Junko Iwamoto on additional consult.
  • Edition pick rationale: DE Carlsen Doppelband chosen over EN VIZ 2-vol mega-HC box on shelf-register grounds — 4-vol-in-slipcase matches the Akira / GitS Legacy / Alita Deluxe / Utena Deluxe pattern AND sits naturally next to the DE-Master-Edition track (Eden / Vagabond / Biomega / Tokyo Ghost). Translation argument was neutral (neither EN nor DE is original-language; Miyazaki was hands-off on Western translations).

Series: Complete 1996–2018 少女 Shōjo

AtR

Box contents & series notes
  • Premise: Utena Tenjou, a girl who adopts princely register (uniform, sword, vow to "become a prince"), arrives at Ohtori Academy and gets pulled into a duel tournament where the prize is Anthy Himemiya, the "Rose Bride" — a girl treated as a possession to be won. The duels and the duelists' motivations gradually reveal a deeper system Utena is fighting to break.
  • Register: critical engagement with patriarchal-heterosexual violence — gender coercion, domestic and child abuse, grooming, rape, incest as subject matter the work seriously critiques, not romanticises. Akio (series villain) is the explicit abuser-figure; the story's argument is against him, not for him. Frank but not graphic.
  • Team: Be-Papas — story collective led by director Kunihiko Ikuhara, including Yoji Enokido and others. Chiho Saito provides the manga's art (classical late-90s shōjo register — flowing line, ornate, ribbon-and-rose iconography). Saito-Be-Papas collaboration is unusual: she illustrates a story conceived by a collective primarily known for the anime.
  • Format: Complete Deluxe Box Set (Viz Media, 2017, ISBN 9781421585871). Two oversized hardcovers — RGU (the original 1996–1998 Ciao serialization, 5 tankobon compressed into 1 HC) + ADO (The Adolescence of Utena, 1999 film-adaptation manga, 1 HC). 1,131 pp total, exclusive color pages, includes poster.
  • After the Revolution: Saito Chiho's 2017–2018 single-volume return to the series (Viz EN edition 2019, ISBN 9781974715145). Three short stories revisiting Utena/Anthy, Juri/Shiori, and Saionji-Touga post-series. Not collected into the Deluxe Box Set (Box predates AtR by two years); separate softcover purchase. Saito-canonical continuation, not a Be-Papas collective work.
  • Content note: depicts abuse / grooming / incest / rape / patriarchal coercion frankly, as the topics being critiqued. Passes content-ethics framework (critical engagement, not romanticisation). Akio is villain not romantic lead. Themes get more prevalent toward the end of the series.

Watchmen
Deluxe Edition HC · EN · DC Comics · Alan Moore / Dave Gibbons / John Higgins
Series: Complete 1986–1987 US
DLX
1 / 1 owned · single-vol Deluxe HC · read
Series notes
  • What & why: Alan Moore + Dave Gibbons + John Higgins (1986–87, 12 issues, DC). Alternate-history 1985 where Nixon's still president, Vietnam was won via Doctor Manhattan, costumed adventurers are real but mostly retired, Cold War about to go hot. Western-GN-canon foundational text alongside Akira and Nausicaä — 1986–87 parallel to the 1982–94 Japanese pair; Tokyo Ghost cites Watchmen as direct influence.
  • Edition: DC 2013 Deluxe HC, oversized, ~25cm tall, matte cover stock — the production-quality the book finally got a decade-plus after the original 12-issue floppy run. Gibbons's 9-panel grid + Higgins's color-coded chapter palette + Moore's symmetrical layout structure all preserved.
  • Content note: Comedian's rape of Silk Spectre I depicted as the trauma it is, not eroticised — Moore indicts him, Sally's later complicated stance shown as damage not redemption. Passes critical-engagement gate. Ozymandias's mass-murder ending treated as horror, not heroism.

  • Umbrella: Mizuno pattern — one-shots, artist is the grouping key. Okazaki is THE 90s josei pioneer; career ended by a 1996 traffic accident.   • RIV — River's Edge (1993–94): flat-affect adolescent nihilism — six high schoolers, a closeted gay boy, a bulimic model, a corpse in the riverbed as shared secret. 18+. Critical engagement with bullying, queerness, eating disorders — harm as harm, passes the gate. Register flag: adolescent problem-set (cf. Wandering Son axis b). 2018 Isao Yukisada film. EN Kodansha 2023, Becky Cloonan cover + footnotes; no DE.
  • HS — Helter Skelter (1995–96): beauty-industry psychological horror — supermodel Liliko's full-body-surgery decay. Tezuka Osamu Cultural Prize Grand Prize 2004; Angoulême Official Selection 2008. DE Carlsen 2005 OOP.
  • Art register: rough anti-pretty fashion-illustration line — deliberate and load-bearing; the opposite of the shelf's Nihei/Inoue/Kishiro draftsmanship.

Artist + edition notes
  • Why an umbrella: Mizuno's catalogue is multi-book but each is a self-contained one-shot. Grouping at the artist level (same author, same signature register) instead of per-book. Sibling Considering entry holds the remaining one-shot (Pelu).
  • Register: "kawaii-noir" / gothic-kawaii — cute character design + transgressive adult content (sex, violence, body horror) rendered in pastel shoujo aesthetics. Female mangaka, Harajuku-90s-underground origin. Rare bullseye on the female-author + cute-with-teeth + power-fantasy axis.
  • Pure Trance (1998): debut. Drug-addicted nurses in an authoritarian underground future; all-female cast, surreal body horror. Originally serialised in Pure Trance techno-compilation CD booklets; collected by East Press 1998. Last Gasp English edition 2005.
  • Cinderalla (2000): fairy-tale retelling — Cinderella as chicken-girl chef. Sex + cannibalism transgressive fairy-tale register. Viz English edition.
  • Princess Mermaid (2002): mermaid sisters running an underwater brothel where they kill and eat the clientele. The Powerpuff-Girls-weaponised pink-revenge pick, same DNA as Pure Trance. Viz English edition.
  • Hansel et Gretel (2002): Mizuno's other Viz fairy-tale retelling, sibling protagonists. IMHO FR edition (same Amazon price as the Viz EN — Gwen took the FR for a deliberate translation-comparison point against the otherwise-EN Mizuno shelf).
  • Ravina the Witch? (2014): the gentler Mizuno — young witch on a journey, cute register, less of the "not holding back" transgression. EN Titan Comics edition chosen (over FR Soleil original) on practical grounds.
  • Canonical bibliography: Mizuno's official products page — authoritative source for what exists in which language and edition. Treat this as the truth-of-record over WebSearch synthesis.

Series notes & pick rationale
  • Premise: Tokyo 2048, Eurasian-Railroad terminus megalopolis gripped by mutilation killings. Origin — a prototype android created by a scientist who's now dead — hides his existence while hunting down his "murderous siblings" (other androids) and learning what it means to be human.
  • Register: cyberpunk seinen with android-identity meditation. The GitS-adjacent philosophical axis (what is it to be human when you're not?) done as a 10-vol serial story. Boichi's art is widely praised — cinematic, dense, polished, dynamic. He's the Dr. Stone illustrator + the Sun-Ken Rock author.
  • Mangaka: Boichi (Korean-born, working in Japan). Young Magazine seinen 2016–2019. Film adaptation announced 2023 (Majestic / Kodansha).
  • Format: Vertical Comics paperback, 10 vols complete in EN as of late 2023. No continuity risk.
  • Why on radar: structurally on-axis — cyberpunk + android identity themes + complete + exceptional art + adjacent to her existing GitS / BLAME! / Eden / Akira shelf. The Boichi-cyberpunk slot.
  • Why low-probability — content gate caveat: Boichi's signature includes recurring fanservice framing of women — "frequently derailing into awkward fan service and tonal whiplash, shifting from philosophical musings to exaggerated comedy or near-hentai shots" per reviewers; "female characters often reduced to objectifying poses that distract from the atmosphere." Different failure mode from Gantz: punctuated fanservice intrusions rather than structurally embedded male-gaze. The work's central concern is the android's philosophy, the female cast isn't the work's romanticised subject — but the intrusion happens often enough to be a documented pattern, not isolated panels. Personal threshold call between "irritating-but-tolerable" and "disruptive-enough-to-ruin." Sits on the radar for re-evaluation; aware, not actively pursuing.

Series notes & edition comparison
  • Premise: Coco apprentices under the witch Qifrey in a world where magic is hidden and craft-controlled. Magical-school coming-of-age fantasy.
  • Register: European-illuminated-manuscript visual register, Vagabond-tier line-detail. Cute is good.
  • Mangaka: Kamome Shirahama (female, illustrator / cover-art background).
  • Honours: won the Eisner for Best US Edition of International Material (Asia) twice — 2020 + 2025.
  • Tankobon track: standard paperback singles, ~5.79 × 8.15″, matte white covers with unique cover art per volume (spines feature individual characters from the cast — assembled spines form one long character lineup). EN at vol 14 latest; vols 15–16 not yet announced for EN despite being out in JP.
  • Grimoire HC track: oversized 7 × 10″ hardcovers, three tankobon per Grimoire, gold-foil-on-blue with intricate frame ornament, rounded spines, sewn binding, matte premium paper (color pages explicitly designed for it), 32 extra bonus pages per Grimoire (~mini art book per volume: B&W + color illustrations, character sketches, casting-seal intro, world-and-animals guide, creator/editor interview, commentary). Mirrors the previously-released French Grimoire format. Same translation as singles.
  • Edition-design-as-signal read: Grimoire's gold-foil-on-blue ornate-frame design aligns with Gwen's premium-HC shelf register. Tankobon design is also nice but more conventional. Grimoire is the on-axis pick.
  • Why considering: female-creator + seinen-demographic + double-Eisner + on-axis art register + premium HC option available = structural standout for the female-author niche.
  • Trade-off if committing to Grimoire: gap risk is low — Witch Hat Atelier is a flagship Kodansha USA license (double Eisner, Trigger anime, active JP run, French Grimoire precedent already proven). Publishers don't launch deluxe lines for series they aren't committed to. What IS real is PACING: Grimoires release ~2 per year, so completing the full set would take 4–5+ years. Tankobon is the "available right now in full" pick; Grimoire is the "premium but slow" pick.
