⌚ Watch Science — What Apple Watch Adds

Phone-on-mattress achieves 80–88% sleep/wake accuracy but stage classification is heuristic (F1 0.26–0.69). Apple Watch adds cardiovascular signals that are orthogonal to movement — heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate, and SpO2. Combined models reach ~77–78% accuracy for 4-stage classification.

Heart Rate Patterns Across Sleep Stages

Research Basis

A deep learning model using instantaneous heart rate achieved 0.77 accuracy and 0.66 Cohen's kappa for 4-class sleep staging (wake/light/deep/REM) on 10,000+ nights.

Source: Radha M et al. (2020) “Deep learning for automated sleep staging using instantaneous heart rate” — npj Digital Medicine

🌑
Deep Sleep (N3)
Minimal HR variation. Slow, stable heart rate. Lowest of all stages. Parasympathetic dominance.
👁
REM Sleep
Higher HR variability, resembles wakefulness. Rapid rate changes. Sympathetic activation.
💤
Light Sleep (N1/N2)
HR slowed from wake but more variable than deep. Transitional pattern.

Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

Key HRV Metrics for Sleep Staging

RMSSD (Root Mean Square of Successive Differences): Primary parasympathetic/vagal marker. Elevated in light sleep, lowest in deep sleep (N3), decreases in REM.

LF/HF ratio (Low Frequency 0.04–0.15 Hz / High Frequency 0.15–0.4 Hz): Higher in wake and REM (sympathetic dominant), lower in NREM (parasympathetic dominant).

SDNN (Standard Deviation of Normal-to-Normal intervals): Global HRV marker. Lower in deep sleep.

HRV Reliability by Stage

Herzig D et al. (2017) found stage-dependent reliability:

  • N2 (light): excellent reliability for HF, LF, RMSSD
  • N3 (deep): lowest RMSSD and LF power; LF unreliable in deep sleep
  • REM: excellent reliability for HF and LF

Sources: Herzig D et al. (2017) “Reproducibility of Heart Rate Variability Is Parameter and Sleep Stage Dependent” — Frontiers in Physiology; Boudreau P et al. (2013) “Circadian variation of heart rate variability across sleep stages” — Sleep

Respiratory Rate

Respiratory Rate as Stage Discriminator

Available from Apple Watch since watchOS 8 (September 2021) via HKQuantityTypeIdentifierRespiratoryRate.

  • Light sleep: Slightly slower than wake
  • Deep sleep: Slow, regular breathing (low coefficient of variation)
  • REM sleep: Irregular, variable respiratory rate — key REM marker

A cardiorespiratory sleep staging algorithm (CReSS) combining HR + respiratory rate achieved Cohen's kappa 0.643 for 4-stage classification (wake/light/deep/REM) and 77.6% epoch-level accuracy.

Source: Sridhar N et al. (2020) “Estimating sleep stages using cardiorespiratory signals: validation of a novel algorithm across demographic groups” — SLEEP

Blood Oxygen (SpO2)

Not for Staging — For Quality & Screening

SpO2 cannot distinguish sleep stages. It is used for sleep quality and disordered-breathing detection.

Normal sleep: SpO2 stable 95–100%, minimal fluctuation.

Oxygen Desaturation Index (ODI): Count of ≥3% SpO2 drops per hour.

  • ODI > 5: suggests mild sleep apnea (5–15 events/hour)
  • ODI > 10: 93% sensitivity, 75% specificity for moderate-to-severe sleep-disordered breathing
  • ODI > 30: severe

Source: PMC article (2023) “Oxygen desaturation index as a diagnostic marker for OSA”

Schlafi should flag, not diagnose. Display warning if ODI suggests evaluation needed.

📐 Watch Maths — Sensor Fusion Formulas

HR-Based Stage Priors

HR_baseline = mean(HR) over all sleep epochs
HR_std = std(HR) over all sleep epochs

Per-epoch HR features:
  HR_z = (HR_epoch − HR_baseline) / HR_std
  HR_var = variance(HR) within epoch (computed from individual samples)

Stage bias from HR:
  Deep:  HR_z < −0.5 (sustained low HR) → P(deep) × 1.5
  REM:   HR_var > P75(session HR variance) → P(REM) × 1.4
  Light: otherwise → no modifier

HRV Stage Priors

Compute per-epoch (30s minimum, ideally 5-min rolling):
  RMSSD = √(mean(ΔRR²)) where ΔRR = successive RR interval differences
  LF/HF = power(0.04–0.15 Hz) / power(0.15–0.4 Hz) from RR interval spectrum

Stage bias from HRV:
  Deep:  RMSSD < P25(session) → P(deep) × 1.5
  REM:   LF/HF > P75(session) → P(REM) × 1.4
  Light: both within P25–P75 → no modifier

Respiratory Rate Modifiers

RR_epoch = breaths per minute (from HealthKit or derived)
RR_CV = coefficient of variation of RR within sliding window

Stage bias from RR:
  REM:  RR_CV > 0.15 → P(REM) × 1.3  (irregular breathing = REM marker)
  Deep: RR_CV < 0.05 → P(deep) × 1.2  (regular, slow breathing)

Sensor Fusion — Updated Stage Probability

Phone-only (existing):
  P(stage) = P_movement(level, stage) × P_cycle(cycle, stage) × position_bias

Phone + Watch:
  P(stage) = P_movement × P_cycle × position_bias × M_HR × M_HRV × M_RR
  where M_x are multiplicative modifiers from watch signals (default 1.0 if no data)

Normalize: P(stage) = P(stage) / Σ P(all stages)
Assign: stage = argmax P(stage)

Confidence Boost with Watch

Watch data increases per-epoch confidence:
  confidence_epoch(watch) ≈ 1.3–1.5 × confidence_epoch(phone-only)

Rationale: HR/HRV provide independent confirmation of stage assignment.
  Movement says “still” → could be deep OR REM.
  HR says “low, stable” → confirms deep.
  HR says “variable, elevated” → confirms REM.

The ambiguity between deep and REM (the main weakness of phone-only)
is directly addressed by cardiovascular signals.
Epoch Length

Phone-only: 60s epochs (noisier signal needs averaging)
Phone + Watch: 30s epochs (watch signal is clean, matches clinical PSG standard)

SpO2 Quality Adjustment

ODI = count(SpO2 drops ≥ 3%) / total_sleep_hours

Quality modifier:
  ODI ≤ 5: no adjustment
  5 < ODI ≤ 10: informational flag (“elevated desaturation events detected”)
  ODI > 10: quality penalty −5 pts + prominent warning (“consider clinical evaluation”)
  ODI > 30: quality penalty −10 pts + strong warning

⚙️ HealthKit Integration

Apple Watch sleep data flows through HealthKit. Schlafi reads cardiovascular signals to improve its classifier, or uses Apple's pre-computed sleep stages directly when available.

Heart Rate

HKQuantityTypeIdentifierHeartRate. Continuous during sleep when watch is worn. Provides instantaneous HR samples for stage discrimination.

Respiratory Rate

HKQuantityTypeIdentifierRespiratoryRate. Available since watchOS 8. Sleeping respiratory rate measurement via micro-motion monitoring.

Blood Oxygen

HKQuantityTypeIdentifierOxygenSaturation. Background SpO2 measurements during sleep. Not available on all watch models.

Sleep Analysis

HKCategoryTypeIdentifierSleepAnalysis. Apple's pre-computed sleep stages (inBed, asleepCore, asleepDeep, asleepREM, awake). When available, Schlafi can use these directly instead of its own classifier.

Dual Path Strategy
Apple Stages Available
  • Use HealthKit sleep categories directly
  • Full confidence — trustworthy stage data
  • Quality scoring uses these stages with full penalties
Raw HR/HRV/RR Only
  • Use watch cardiovascular data to improve phone-based classifier
  • Sensor fusion via multiplicative modifiers
  • Higher confidence than phone-only

No watch data: Fall back to phone-only heuristic classifier with confidence attenuation.
