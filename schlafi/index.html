⚡ TL;DR

Schlafi is a sleep tracker for iOS that uses your phone's accelerometer to detect sleep, estimate stages, and score quality — no wearable required. Place your phone on the mattress and let physics do the rest.

📱

Phone-on-Mattress Tracking

Your phone's accelerometer detects body movements transmitted through the mattress. Spring mattresses work best; foam and latex work too. No wearable needed.

📊

Sleep/Wake Detection

Clinically validated algorithms (Sadeh 1994, Cole-Kripke 1992) classify sleep vs wake with 80–88% accuracy — comparable to wrist actigraphy.

🌌

Stage Estimation

Heuristic classification of REM, light, deep, and awake periods using movement intensity and ultradian cycle timing. Best-effort, not clinical-grade — honest about limits.

⭐

Quality Scoring

Sleep quality score based on duration and phase distribution. Reuses the proven SleepQualityService from OmniWidget. Watch data upgrades accuracy when available.

⌚

Watch Data

Apple Watch heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate, and SpO2 add orthogonal cardiovascular signals. Improves stage classification from heuristic to ~77% accuracy. HealthKit integration — works with any watch that writes sleep data.

🎤

Sound Analysis

Optional passive microphone listening detects snoring (98% accuracy), breathing patterns, and environmental noise. All processing on-device, no audio uploaded.

🧠

On-Device ML

Apple Watch ML for sleep stages, respiratory rate, and apnea screening. Classical algorithms for phone sleep/wake and audio breathing analysis. All on-device, no cloud.
