⚡ TL;DR

Powerli gives you the battery widgets iOS never shipped. See your charge history, connected device levels, screen-on time, and battery health — all at a glance on your home screen.

Battery Timeline — hero widget
  • Battery timeline. Line chart of your battery level over the last hours — see drain patterns at a glance.
  • Configurable device grid. Pick which connected devices to show — both AirPods but not the case, just your watch, whatever you want.
  • Screen-on time. Hours of actual screen use since last charge — the stat Apple removed in iOS 26.
  • Charging speed. Current watts and estimated time to full, right on your home screen.
  • Charge cycles. Daily charge cycle count over the past week as a bar chart.
  • Battery health trend. Maximum capacity % over months, not just today's single number.
  • Power view. Real-time energy consumption like macOS Activity Monitor — but as a widget.
