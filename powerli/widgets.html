🔋 Powerli

"Power, at a glance."

The battery app iOS should have shipped. Real-time charge charts, connected device batteries you can actually configure, screen-on time, charging speed, and health trends — all as widgets.

iPhone iPad
TL;DR Widgets

⭐ Hero Widgets

Battery Timeline

Line chart + battery icon

Shows battery level over the last 6 hours as a smooth line chart. A battery icon on the right displays current charge at a glance. Charging periods are marked with a lightning bolt.

Small Medium

Device Grid

Configurable connected devices

A 2×2 grid showing battery levels for your connected devices. You choose which devices appear — show both AirPods but skip the case, add your Watch but not your Mac. Color-coded by charge level: green, amber, red.

Medium Large
