⚡ TL;DR

Scali is a weight tracker for iOS. You log your weight, and the app turns it into beautiful charts with rolling averages, trend lines, and smart status emojis that tell you how you’re doing at a glance.

The Weight Chart — your data, beautifully
  • Beautiful weight charts. 7-day and 14-day rolling averages with trend lines and gradient fills.
  • Smart status emojis. ✨ when you hit your target zone, 😊 when trending right, ⚠️🚨 when drifting — works for both gaining and losing goals.
  • Target tracking. Set your goal, see your journey from origin to target on every widget.
  • Two color themes. Rose/Red (default) or Ocean/Cyan — pick what feels right.
  • Watch complications. Current weight, trend arrow, and mini charts on your wrist.
  • 100% free. Everything included — no tiers, no premium, no in-app purchases.
  • HealthKit native. Part of the Gwen Widgets ecosystem, sharing HealthWidgetsCore.
