🎯 Vision & Positioning

Weight tracking apps are everywhere. Beautiful weight visualization is not. Gwichti exists because weight data deserves better than a number in a list. Your 7-day average, your 14-day trend, your journey from start to goal — all rendered as charts you actually want to look at, on widgets you’ll glance at fifty times a day.

Target audience: People who weigh themselves and want to see their progress beautifully, without the noise of calorie counting, workout tracking, or meal planning.

Name: “Gwichti” — from Gewicht (German for weight). Short, memorable, international.

⭐ Core Features

⚖️

Weight Tracking

  • Read from HealthKit (bodyMass) — syncs with Apple Health and other apps
  • Write user-entered weights back to HealthKit
  • Simple add/edit/remove interface — date picker, weight input, done
  • Daily weight, 7-day rolling average, 14-day rolling average computed automatically
🎯

Target & Origin

  • Set target weight — where you’re heading
  • Set origin date — where you started (auto-reads weight at that date; optional manual override)
  • Progress percentage, remaining delta, trend projection
  • Bidirectional — works for weight loss AND weight gain goals
🎨

Configuration

  • Color scheme toggle: Rose/Red or Ocean/Cyan (live preview)
  • Units: kg or lbs (auto-converts all displays)
  • Cute Mode toggle
  • Widget gallery (preview each widget before adding to home screen)
📱

The App — Inspired by “My Weight”

Gwichti’s app takes cues from “My Weight” (the iOS app) but stays even simpler — one screen, no tab bar.

Single scroll view:

  • Summary card at top: today’s weight (big number), total delta from origin (e.g., “↓ 5.7 kg” with colored arrow), days since origin. Like “My Weight”’s top card.
  • Chart: the hero visual, same rendering as the medium widget (7d/14d averages, trend line, gradient fill). Period selector (2W / 1M / 3M / All) like “My Weight”’s quarter selector.
  • Trend insight below chart: “Losing X% per month” / “On track to reach your goal by [date]” — simple, motivating.
  • Today section: current entry row (weight, delta from yesterday, time). Tap to edit. Chevron for detail.
  • Recent entries: last 7 days as compact rows. Tap to edit, swipe to delete.
  • Large FAB (“+” button): opens a clean sheet — date picker (defaults to now) + weight numeric input. Done.

No tab bar. Just the weight screen + settings gear in nav bar. The simplicity IS the feature. “My Weight” has 4 tabs (Weight, Data, Widgets, Settings); Gwichti has one screen because it does less — and that’s the point.

Entry editing: Tap any row → same sheet as add, pre-filled. No confirmation dialogs.

Status Emojis — Bidirectional

EmojiMeaningWhen
✨In the target zoneWithin 0.5 kg of target. Shows on entry day and today. Past days in-zone show nothing — keeps the sparkle earned, not boring.
😊Trending toward targetWeight is moving the right direction vs your averages.
(empty)NeutralSlight movement or sustained in-zone.
⚠️Drifting away0.3–0.5 kg wrong direction vs average.
🚨Significantly away≥0.5 kg wrong direction.

Direction flips automatically based on your goal: if you’re losing, being below average is 😊; if you’re gaining, being above average is 😊. The app figures it out from your origin and target.

Note: This is a global Gwen Widgets change — also benefits OmniWidget. Gwichti has NO workout award emojis (💪🎀😴). Weight data only.
