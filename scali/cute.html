💗 Cute Mode

A single toggle in Settings — from clean to adorable

Rule: “Every heart has a job.” Hearts serve as backdrops, markers, separators, and brand stamps — never random decoration. UX first, cute second.

🚫 Clean (OFF)

No hearts. Standard dots, lines, circles. Minimal UI, zero heart imagery.

💗 Adorable (ON)

Hearts woven into the UI. Chart dots become heart markers. Progress arc endpoints become hearts. Widget corners get brand stamps. Heart color matches your chosen theme — 💗 rose or 💙 ocean.
