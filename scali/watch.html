⌚ Watch App

The Scali watch app is a lightweight companion for glanceable weight data.

Current Weight

Big number with trend arrow and status emoji. The watch face of Scali.

📈

7-Day Mini Chart

Spark line showing your last week’s weight trend. Quick visual: are you heading the right direction?

🎯

Target Progress

Arc gauge showing how far you’ve come from origin to target. Percentage + remaining.

✏️

Quick-Log Entry

Digital Crown to adjust weight value, tap to confirm and save to HealthKit. Fast morning weigh-in from the wrist.

📚 Smart Stack

Scali surfaces in the watchOS Smart Stack when weight was logged recently (morning weigh-in habit), when approaching the target zone, or based on daily patterns. Uses RelevanceConfiguration (watchOS 26+, @available-gated) to provide contextual relevance hints to the system.
